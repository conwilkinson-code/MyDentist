Adults seen: practice location vs where people live

The analysis's "adults seen" figures match NHSBSA's count by practice location: patients seen at practices in a council area, divided by that area's adult population. That inflates areas whose practices draw patients from next door and deflates their neighbours. The ward map uses the residents' measure: the share of adults living in each area who saw an NHS dentist in the 24 months to March 2025.

Practice count check

Plotting each practice by its postcode puts six councils on a different count from the analysis. mydentist names some practices after a nearby larger town rather than the council they sit in. The tiers and figures above still use the analysis counts; the last column shows what the capacity ratio becomes on the postcode count.
