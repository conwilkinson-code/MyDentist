mydentist Yorkshire & Humber — Opportunity Map

Three measures combined per local authority: child dental need (NDEP oral health survey 2023/24), mydentist practice capacity per capita, and NHS access (% adults seen by an NHS dentist in the last 24 months, NHSBSA 2024/25). Select an area for its breakdown.

Grey areas are outside the analysis. North Yorkshire is shown as the single council area created in 2023.
