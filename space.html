mydentist Yorkshire & Humber — Opportunity Map

Three data layers combined per local authority: child dental need (NDEP oral health survey 2023/24), mydentist practice capacity per capita, and NHS access (% adults seen by an NHS dentist in the last 24 months, NHSBSA 2024/25). Click any marker for the full breakdown.

Legend

Priority opportunityPoor on need, capacity and/or access — strongest case for investment.
Mixed / data gapStrong signal on one or two measures, but a data gap (usually no oral health survey participation) prevents a full picture.
Well servedAdequate or above-average across the measures we have data for.
Sources: NHSBSA Dental Statistics England 2024/25 (patients seen, geographical aggregations); OHID National Dental Epidemiology Programme, oral health survey of 5-year-old schoolchildren 2023/24; mydentist practice locator (mydentist.co.uk), scraped July 2026; ONS Census 2021 (population estimates). Practices-per-capita uses 5-year-old population as a consistent cross-area proxy, not a literal patient denominator. Four local authorities (East Riding, North Yorkshire, Wakefield, York) did not participate in the 2023/24 oral health survey, so decay figures are unavailable for them.
